# Kill Criteria

Kill criteria are product features, not pessimism. Their purpose is to stop sunk-cost escalation before downstream infrastructure exists.

## Stage 0 — Foundation

Block advancement if:
- clean checkout cannot run the documented bootstrap/check command;
- a second runtime language is required without demonstrated need;
- CI needs mutable action tags or broad token permissions;
- a known shortcut is required to make checks pass;
- a direct dependency lacks a documented purpose.

## Stage 1 — Source qualification

Candidate quality gates to freeze before measurement:
- successful parse rate >=99.5%;
- schema violations <=0.5%;
- coverage of the explicitly defined market-legible universe >=95%;
- p95 discovery latency <=120 seconds;
- first-buyer availability >=95% of eligible assets;
- audited first-buyer identity/order >=99% agreement with direct-chain truth;
- zero undocumented semantic shifts during the qualification packet.

If no affordable source stack can meet the frozen gates, kill V0 before permanent storage/detector work.

## Stage 2 — Temporal ledger

Stop if:
- replay from raw observations is not deterministic;
- duplicate ingest changes semantic state;
- a future-fetched observation can enter a past snapshot;
- current projection cannot be rebuilt from raw facts;
- raw observations require mutation/deletion to stay correct.

## Stage 4 — Structural actor kernel

Pivot/kill if:
- direct-chain audit materially disagrees with provider actor identity/order;
- most overlap vanishes once known service/high-ubiquity actors are removed;
- actor overlap is dominated by ambiguous infrastructure accounts.

## Stage 5 — Formation calibration

Kill the Formation thesis if, under the frozen calibration protocol:
- largest connected component repeatedly contains >10% of active universe because of generic bridges;
- >50% of candidate formations depend on one actor;
- >50% are effectively one deployer;
- formations disappear after reasonable high-ubiquity filtering;
- trivial name/deployer clustering is equally or more informative;
- formation rate is operationally useless at reasonable frozen parameters.

Parameter percentages are calibration gates, not universal scientific constants; any change must be logged before preregistration.

## Stage 6 — Attention qualification

Kill `SILENT_IGNITION` if:
- obvious loud examples are incorrectly THIN >5%;
- required information-family coverage <95%;
- `UNAVAILABLE -> THIN` occurs even once in qualification tests;
- token symbol/name collisions cannot be controlled reproducibly;
- no source family materially observes the crypto discourse the product claims to measure.

A failed attention instrument does not automatically kill the Formation product; it kills the “before the timeline” claim.

## Stage 7/8 — Research freeze/prospective cohort

Invalidate the cohort if a lookahead path affects any decision. Preserve the failed cohort, fix under a new detector version, and start a new cohort.

Kill `SILENT_IGNITION_V0` at the preregistered stopping boundary if the primary endpoint fails to exceed the preregistered baseline by the minimum useful effect with the preregistered uncertainty criterion.

Do not rescue it by moving thresholds after seeing outcomes.

## Stage 10 — Operator utility

- >=60% consecutive alerts worth opening: advance;
- 40–60%: exactly one bounded presentation/information-density revision and one re-test;
- <40%: kill the alert product;
- second failure after bounded revision: kill.

## Stage 11 — Notifications

Block launch if any covered crash/retry scenario can lose a committed alert or create an externally duplicated publication without deterministic reconciliation.

## Stage 12 — Independent receipts

No “verifiable receipt” claim if:
- TypeScript/Rust canonical digests disagree;
- a bound-field mutation passes verification;
- verifier requires detector/provider code to reconstruct a receipt;
- original source/config/code identities are not bound.

## Stage 14 — Ember

Kill economic Ember if:
- role assignment is unstable under small defensible perturbations;
- forward lift disappears against preregistered matched controls;
- lift exists only in calibration data used to invent the role;
- realistic spread/fees/liquidity constraints eliminate the useful effect;
- success depends on selecting a winner from many variants without surviving multiple-testing/selection correction.
