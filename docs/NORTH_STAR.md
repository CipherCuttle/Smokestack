# Smokestack North Star v0

## Mission

Smokestack identifies structurally related groups of newly active Solana assets and tests whether those formations precede qualified public attention around their members.

It is not an oracle, recommendation engine, or trading bot. Its first job is to determine whether the underlying information relation exists at all.

## Product sentence

> **Something non-trivial appears to be organizing onchain. Here is exactly why, what was knowable when it fired, whether the public had noticed, and what happened next.**

## Core ontology

| Object | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Smoke | Raw point-in-time observation from a qualified source |
| Formation | Multiple assets connected by frozen structural evidence |
| Tripwire | A frozen divergence decision worth inspection |
| Blackline | Minimal factual explanation of why a Tripwire fired |
| Torch | Later role: mature representative of a validated Formation |
| Ember | Later research role: strongly connected, comparatively immature member |
| Clone | Later role: narrative/identity relation without structural support |
| Trap | Later role: connected member with materially adverse structural evidence |
| Receipt | Content-addressed pre-outcome evidence binding |
| Ash | Preserved false alarm or dead Formation |

## Claim ladder

Smokestack must earn claims in order. No later claim may be made because an earlier one “seems plausible.”

1. **Observation claim** — the chosen market universe can be observed with adequate coverage and freshness.
2. **Temporal claim** — Smokestack can reconstruct what was knowable at decision time without future leakage.
3. **Actor claim** — early participant identities can be measured reliably enough to use as neutral structural evidence.
4. **Formation claim** — non-trivial formations exist after service/high-ubiquity/deployer contamination controls.
5. **Incremental claim** — those formations contain information beyond trivial name/deployer clustering.
6. **Attention claim** — public member attention can be qualified as THIN/PRESENT/SATURATED, and source outage never masquerades as THIN.
7. **Silent Ignition claim** — formations with THIN public attention prospectively lead attention transitions better than preregistered controls by a minimum useful effect.
8. **Operator utility claim** — consecutive alerts are worth inspecting often enough to justify a product surface.
9. **Verification claim** — public receipts can be independently reproduced and tampering is detected.
10. **Ember claim** — in a separate prospective study, relative immaturity inside validated formations has robust economic information after controls and frictions.
11. **Cross-chain claim** — a second chain can implement the contracts without contaminating core semantics.

## Initial detector

Technical study name: `FORMATION_LOW_PUBLIC_MEMBER_ATTENTION_V0`.

User-facing name, only after efficacy survives: `SILENT_IGNITION`.

V0 Formation evidence is intentionally simple and non-semantic:

- temporal proximity;
- qualifying early-actor overlap;
- independent deployers;
- structural non-junk facts.

Semantic labels may be generated only after a Formation snapshot is frozen and may never change membership or the detector decision.

## North-star falsification table

| Claim | Required evidence | Kill condition |
|---|---|---|
| We can see the universe early enough | qualified coverage/freshness audit | source stack cannot meet frozen coverage/latency requirements at acceptable cost |
| We know what was knowable at T | append-only temporal ledger + replay tests | future observation can affect sealed past state or replay is non-deterministic |
| Early actors are reliable observations | provider-vs-chain audit | material identity/order disagreement |
| Non-trivial formations exist | calibrated deterministic graph | formations are mainly one deployer, service accounts, or graph percolation |
| Formations add information | baseline comparison | no useful increment over name/deployer baseline |
| Public attention is observable | qualified social + independent information family | cannot distinguish obvious loud/quiet cases; UNAVAILABLE leaks into THIN |
| Silent Ignition leads attention | frozen prospective cohort | fails preregistered minimum useful effect |
| Alerts are useful | consecutive blinded operator review | <40% worth opening; 40–60% gets at most one bounded revision |
| Receipts are independently reproducible | independent verifier | digest mismatch or bound-field tamper passes |
| Ember has economic value | separate prospective economic study | no robust controlled lift after frictions/selection correction |
| Architecture generalizes | second adapter | chain-specific hacks invade core contracts |

## Absolute boundaries

- Governance and reproducibility do not create signal.
- `RECURRENT_ACTOR` never means `SMART_MONEY` without separate evidence.
- `FORMATION_MEMBER` never means `GOOD_ASSET`.
- `THIN` never means “provider returned nothing.”
- Missing evidence never silently becomes negative evidence.
- No historical success can rewrite a pre-outcome snapshot.
- Every false alarm remains part of the lineage.
